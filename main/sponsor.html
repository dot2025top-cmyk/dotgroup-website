Before You Register, Meet Your Sponsor!

You need a sponsor before you can register with DOTGROUP.

Your sponsor is the person who introduces you to DOTGROUP, helps you understand our platform, and provides your personal registration link.

How to Get Started

  1. Find a sponsor: Connect with a DOTGROUP member who can introduce you to our community.
  2. Request your registration link: Ask your sponsor to send you their personal DOTGROUP registration link.
  3. Open your link: Use the link provided by your sponsor to access the registration form.
  4. Complete your registration: Enter your correct details and follow the instructions on the registration page.

Don't Have a Sponsor Yet?

Don't worry! Contact the DOTGROUP team for assistance in finding a sponsor.
