🔐 Privacy, Security & Consent

Health data is sensitive

A live platform must collect only necessary information and protect it with appropriate access controls and encryption.

Clear consent

Members must understand how their information is used, who can access it and how consent can be withdrawn where applicable.

Separate permissions

Healthcare professionals should access only the records necessary for their authorised care duties. Shop, affiliate and unrelated administrators must not automatically access health records.

Secure account access

Production implementation should include strong authentication, secure sessions, role-based permissions, audit logs and account recovery protections.

Data control

Members need a clear way to request access to their records, corrections, exports or deletion when legally permitted.
